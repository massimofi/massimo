  • Placed 2nd of 10 teams delivering a data-driven $100M capital allocation strategy across healthcare access, capacity, and workforce levers.
  • Engineered a weighted 3-pillar Strain Index combining burden score (recovery, improvement, mortality, DALYs), capacity gap (doctors, beds, healthcare access), and affordability gap (treatment cost vs income) to score country-level need.
  • Built a Random Forest regressor (400 decision trees) on healthcare data across 20 countries and 24 years (2000–2024), trained on years ≤2022 and tested on 2023+.
  • Intentionally excluded burden signals from model features to ensure the model only used inputs that could be changed with capital (access, doctors, beds, income, cost-to-income ratio).
  • Designed a greedy ROI optimizer that deployed $100M across 81 investment decisions, selecting the highest strain-reduction-per-dollar lever at each iteration.
  • Identified country-specific optimal interventions: access investment for Brazil and Saudi Arabia, beds for Italy, doctors for Japan; South Africa saw the largest strain improvement at -0.104.
  • Presented investment strategy and marginal impact framework to panel of judges, placing 2nd of 10 competing teams.

Massimo DCF — Full-Stack DCF Valuation Platform
Personal Project
Nov 2025 – Present
  • Building an end-to-end DCF valuation platform for 500 US public equities, combining custom financial modeling with a real-time web application.
  • Engineered a custom DCF engine (WACC via CAPM, 5-year unlevered FCF projection, Gordon Growth terminal value) that handles financials and REITs separately since revenue-driven DCF doesn't fit their statements.
  • Implemented a 10,000-path Monte Carlo simulation using triangular distributions and WACC × growth sensitivity heatmaps to quantify valuation uncertainty.
  • Auto-derives per-stock DCF assumptions from 3-year historical CAGRs and sector medians, with a fallback chain that handles missing data and NaN values gracefully.
  • Built the backend in Python 3.12 with FastAPI, using Server-Sent Events for streaming pipeline progress and WebSockets for 3-second live price ticks.
  • Ingests SEC 10-K/10-Q filings via edgartools and market data (prices, beta, 10-year Treasury) via yfinance, caching results in PostgreSQL 18.
  • Designed the frontend in React 18 + TypeScript + Vite with Tailwind CSS; used GSAP, Motion, and Lenis for an editorial, scroll-driven UI.
  • Built data visualizations in Recharts (sensitivity heatmaps, Monte Carlo distributions); command palette via cmdk; typography in Playfair Display and Geist Sans/Mono.
  • Planned deployment: Railway (backend + Postgres) and Vercel (frontend) for a live publicly-accessible platform.
